Generate Your Ecojoiner

Use our system to generate custom 2D carpentry specs and 3D CNC and printer files for your bottle.

Heads up — this generator is still in development. You can fill everything out to explore the flow, but spec files can't be generated just yet. Hold tight!
1 Select your bottle Choose the perfect local bottle for your ecojoiner

Choosing the correct bottle type for your turtle (and future constructions with turtles and bottles!) is key. Not all bottles are ideal. The best tend to be tall and thin, rather than short and stubby. Crucial is that the bottles have short top and bottom tappers and have bodies with flat, upright sides. You can use any bottle size, but for the moment we are recommending 1.5L bottles that are between 83mm and 87mm in diameter. Our research shows that type of generic water bottle size is pretty consistent around the world.

2 Set your bottle specs Specify the key dimensions of your bottle

Bottles come in all shapes and sizes. Ecojoiners must adapt! Please measure carefully the key dimensions of your bottle and set them here. All dimensions are in millimetres (mm).

3 Set your material specs Ecojoiners can be built from just about anything flat

Ecojoiners are best built with organic material that is between 8mm and 25mm thick. The best for ocean durability is solid wood boards. Do not use plastic as it will degrade into polluting the ocean and shore. Avoid metal as it will decrease buoyancy.

4 Select fabrication Choose how you'll be fabricating your ecojoiners

Ecojoiners can be made in all sorts of ways. With your selected specs we will generate the file type you need to move ahead with carpentry, 3D printing, laser cutting or CNCing.

  • Carpentry 2D cutting dimensions (PDF)
  • 3D printing 3D file (STL)
  • CNC & laser For CNC machines and laser cutters (DXF)
  • Vector patterns Editable and traceable 1:1 carpenter patterns (SVG)
5 Select ecojoiner type Pick which ecojoiner you want to build

There are more and more types of ecojoiners to choose from.
